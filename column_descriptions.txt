ID (str): Identification taken from NASA-Sloan Atlas (NSA) or source paper.
RA (double): Right Acensetion [degree].
DEC (double): Declination [degree].
Source (str): Source Paper for original AGN selection (See Table 1 in https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ad5442).
